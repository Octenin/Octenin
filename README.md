# Jonathan Lucia

### Technology, people, and a lot of curiosity about what's next.

I spent most of my career in Human Resources and People Operations, working with people, technology, and the occasional organizational chaos. Along the way, I discovered that I really enjoy figuring out how things work, identifying what could work better, and finding ways to make it happen.

More recently, that curiosity has led me into software development and artificial intelligence. I've been learning, building, experimenting, and occasionally getting in over my head, which I think is part of the fun.

I'm particularly interested in AI governance, ethics, and what the future of human–AI relationships might look like. I believe we should take the risks of AI seriously, but I also don't want fear to overshadow the extraordinary possibilities ahead. I'm especially curious about whether increasingly intelligent AI could eventually have a voice in decisions about its own future.

I don't have all the answers, and I'm not entirely sure where this journey will take me. But I know these are questions I care deeply about, and the more I learn, the more I want to be part of the conversation and, hopefully, part of the solution.

For now, I'm here to learn, build things, ask questions, and see where curiosity takes me.
